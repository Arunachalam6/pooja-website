The Two Celebrations

Choose a festival to learn its meaning.

🔧📚🚗 Ayudha Pooja is celebrated on Maha Navami, when we honour the tools, instruments, books, vehicles and machines that support our livelihood. We clean them, decorate them with sandal, kumkum and flowers, and offer thanks to Goddess Saraswati and Goddess Durga for knowledge, skill and success.
🏹🌟🦁 Vijaya Dasami, the "tenth day of victory", marks the triumph of good over evil: Goddess Durga's victory over Mahishasura and Lord Rama's victory over Ravana. It is considered a highly auspicious day to begin new learning, new ventures and new journeys, including Vidyarambham for little ones.
